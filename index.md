# Navinx

Navinx is a free music player for iPhone that plays the music on your own Navidrome or Subsonic server. It doesn't supply any music of its own and has no accounts of its own: you sign in to your server with the login you already have there, and Navinx plays what is on it.

## What you need

A Navidrome server, or another server that speaks the Subsonic API, that you or a friend run, and a user name and password on it.
